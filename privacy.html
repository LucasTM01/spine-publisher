Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Spine Publisher is an internal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google, Spine Publisher receives permission to upload videos to that channel (youtube.upload), to read the channel and the status of its uploads (youtube.readonly) and to read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly).

How the data is used and stored

  • The access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computers and used only to upload our videos and read their status and statistics.
  • Video statistics are stored in the owner's private database to decide what to produce next.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Spine Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored tokens then stop working and are deleted on the next reconnect or on request.
